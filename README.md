## 🌟 Overview / نبذة عن المنصة

**MA TradeLab** is an ultra-high performance Windows desktop workstation engineered specifically for professional futures, forex, and equities traders, as well as proprietary firm (Prop Firm) traders (Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, MyFundedFutures, FTMO). It unifies an **advanced trade journaling engine**, a **real-time prop compliance risk guardian**, a **pre-trade execution gatekeeper**, an **economic calendar with studio voice alerts**, a **comprehensive personal & trading financial ledger**, and **zero-latency local platform connectors**—all running 100% offline with zero external cloud dependencies.

**MA TradeLab** هو محطة عمل مكتبية فائقة الأداء لنظام تشغيل ويندوز، صُممت خصيصاً للمتداولين المحترفين ومتداولي شركات التمويل (Prop Firms) في أسواق الفيوتشرز (Futures)، الفوركس (Forex)، والأسهم. تجمع المنصة بين **سجل صفقات ذكي متقدم**، **محرك حماية والتزام صارم بشروط شركات التمويل**، **محرك تقييم وحساب المخاطر قبل تنفيذ أي صفقة**، **مفكرة اقتصادية فورية مع تنبيهات صوتية مسجلة باستوديو احترافي**، **نظام إدارة مالية شخصية ومالية تداول متكامل**، و**ربط مباشر عبر Localhost مع أشهر منصات التداول (MT5, NinjaTrader 8, ATAS)**—مع ضمان الخصوصية الكاملة 100% بدون أي خوادم سحابية أو جمع بيانات.

## 💎 Core Capabilities / المميزات والقدرات الأساسية

### 1. Smart Trading Journal & Statistical Analytics (سجل الصفقات والتحليل الإحصائي)
- **Multi-Asset Trade Journaling**: Log trades across CME/NYMEX Futures (NQ, ES, YM, RTY, GC, CL, etc.), Spot Forex, and Equities with contract sizing, exact tick calculations, commissions, and gross/net realized PnL.
- **Advanced Mathematical Performance Metrics**:
  - **Expectancy & Expectancy Ratio**: Calculate the true statistical edge per dollar risked.
  - **Win Rate, Profit Factor, & Sharpe Ratio**: Real-time evaluation of strategy quality.
  - **Average Win / Loss & Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE) / Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE)**.
  - **Consecutive Wins / Losses & Drawdown Duration Tracker**.
- **Visual Trade Archiving**: Attach before & after multi-timeframe chart screenshots, entry trigger reasoning, trade behavioral ratings, and emotional discipline notes.
- **Multi-Dimensional Slice & Dice Filtering**: Instantly filter trades by Account, Trading Strategy, Session (New York AM/PM, London, Asia), Symbol, Direction (Long/Short), and Custom Date Ranges.

### 2. Prop Firm Intelligence Engine (PFIE) (محرك ذكاء وشركات التمويل)
- **Built-in Catalog for 50+ Global Prop Firms**: Pre-configured rule parameters for Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, MyFundedFutures, Fast Track Funder, FTMO, FundedNext, and more.
- **Automated Rule Guardrails & Threshold Alerts**:
  - **Daily Loss Limit (DLL)**: Dynamic real-time calculation with warning buffers before breaching accounts.
  - **Trailing Drawdown & Static Drawdown**: Exact tracking against high-water marks and peak balance.
  - **Maximum Loss Limit (MLL)**: Strict risk ceiling visualization.
  - **Profit Target & Consistency Tracking**: Live progress bar toward evaluation completion and payout eligibility.
- **Multi-Account Matrix**: Seamlessly manage and isolate multiple Evaluation challenges, Funded / Master accounts, and Personal accounts simultaneously with zero database cross-contamination.

### 3. Pre-Trade Decision Engine & Risk Management (محرك القرار وإدارة المخاطر قبل التنفيذ)
- **Objective Pre-Trade Checklist**: Protect your capital against FOMO, revenge trading, and emotional impulse entries by executing an objective, multi-variable criteria check before clicking buy or sell.
- **Weighted Rule Checklist**: Build customized strategy rules with percentage weights (0-100%) and non-negotiable mandatory conditions.
- **Automated Contract Sizing & Dollar Risk Calculation**: Enter your stop-loss in points/pips/ticks and account risk percentage; the engine calculates the exact number of contracts/lots to risk.
- **Strict Execution Verdict**: Clear deterministic recommendation (**PASS / نفّذ** or **REJECT / لا تنفّذ**) based on meeting the minimum required strategy score.

### 4. Economic Calendar & Studio Audio Alerts (المفكرة الاقتصادية والتنبيهات الصوتية الحية)
- **Real-Time Macroeconomic Event Feed**: High-impact news tracking for CPI, Core CPI, NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls), FOMC Rate Decisions, PPI, GDP, Retail Sales, and Unemployment Claims.
- **High-Fidelity Studio Human Voice Alerts**:
  - Real human voice countdown reminders: 15 minutes, 5 minutes, 1 minute before high-impact news releases.
  - Second-of-release warning chime to protect open trades from extreme slippage and volatility spikes.
  - Actual outcome notifications (Higher / Lower / In-Line with consensus).
- **Customizable Audio & Language Controls**: Bilingual Arabic & English studio voices with individual event toggles, volume sliders, and sound testing.

### 5. Personal & Trading Financial Management (الإدارة المالية الشاملة للتداول والشخصية)
*(New in Version 2.3.0 / ميزة رئيسية جديدة في الإصدار 2.3.0)*
- **Multi-Account Ledger**: Track Bank Accounts, Physical Cash Reserves, Broker Capital, and Crypto Wallets with full balance synchronization.
- **Strict Double-Entry Accounting Integrity**: Internal transfers between accounts (e.g. Bank to Broker) are isolated and never falsely categorized as income or expenses.
- **Dedicated Trading Cost & Subscription Tracker**:
  - Track live market data fees (CME Level 1 / Level 2), charting platform licenses (TradingView, Bookmap, Sierra Chart), VPS servers, and prop firm fees.
  - Monitor trading education, courses, hardware upgrades (PCs, Monitors, UPS), and equipment maintenance.
- **Subscription Expiry & Renewal Reminders**: Dynamic alert window (1 to 30 days before renewal) with color-coded status badges (`DUE SOON`, `Due Today!`, `Overdue`).
- **Assets & Liabilities Ledger**: Track physical and digital assets vs debts, credit cards, and loans with live Net Worth (صافي الثروة) and Debt-to-Asset ratio calculations.
- **Executive Financial Analytics & 5 Native Vector Charts**:
  1. **Monthly Cash Flow**: Income vs Expense column comparison.
  2. **Net Worth Growth Trajectory**: Cumulative wealth progression polygon.
  3. **Expense Allocation**: Proportional categorical breakdown.
  4. **Trading Performance Ratios**: Trading revenue vs operating trading expenses.
  5. **Savings Progress**: Monthly saved capital vs target goals.
- **Full Editing & Cascade Deletion**: Easily edit account details and safely delete accounts with automated cascade cleanup, preventing database constraint errors.

### 6. Direct Platform Connectors (الربط المباشر مع منصات التداول)
- **Localhost Zero-Latency IPC**: Connects directly to desktop trading platforms without cloud intermediaries:
  - **MetaTrader 5 (MT5)**: MQL5 Expert Advisor (`MATradeLabConnector.mq5`) listening on port 18082 for instant 1-click execution sync.
  - **NinjaTrader 8 (NT8)**: Native C# add-on on port 18080 for live futures trade journaling.
  - **ATAS Platform**: Integrated strategy connector (`MATradeLabAtasConnector.cs`) on port 18081 for order-flow trade logging.

### 7. DeepCharts & MenthorQ Gamma Levels Converter (محول مستويات الجاما المؤسسية)
- **Institutional Option Gamma Integration**: Parse and convert raw options positioning, Call/Put Walls, 0DTE Volatility Triggers, and Gamma Flips from MenthorQ into standardized symbol formats (`$NQ1!`, `$ES1!`, `$QQQ`, `$SPY`, etc.).
- **One-Click DeepCharts Export**: Generate optimized JSON data ready for direct import into DeepCharts for institutional market structure analysis.

### 8. Live Trading Radio (راديو المتداول المباشر)
- **Integrated Background Audio**: Multi-channel streaming including market news feeds, economic radio, Quran recitation, and ambient focus streams (Chill, Lo-Fi, Ambient, Groove Salad) designed to sustain focus and emotional discipline during intense market sessions.

## 🔒 Security & Data Privacy / الأمان والخصوصية

- **100% Offline Architecture (محلي بدون إنترنت)**: جميع بيانات صفقاتك، صور الشارتات، حساباتك المالية، وملاحظاتك الشخصية تُخزن محلياً على جهازك داخل قاعدة بيانات SQLite مشفرة في مجلد `%APPDATA%\MATradeLab`.
- **Zero Cloud Middlemen**: لا يتم إرسال أي صفقة أو بيان مالي إلى أي خادم خارجي أو طرف ثالث نهائياً.
- **Windows DPAPI Protection**: حماية وتشفير بيانات النظام والاعتمادات باستخدام تشفير ويندوز الأصلي على مستوى العتاد.
